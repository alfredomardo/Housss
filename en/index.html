The problem

The usual model is broken

A conventional house takes months to build and years of debt to pay off. It is tied to one plot of land: if you move, the house stays behind.

HOUSSS takes a different route. A small, light home made of repeated parts, built in a workshop, shipped flat and put together with no wet construction and no special tools.

The system

Panels, modules and an Allen key

Everything is joined with Allen bolts into rivet nuts, and with wing nuts. Nothing is glued or welded on site, which is why it can be taken apart and rebuilt.

  • Supports14 levelling feet with rubber pads. No drilling into the slab or the ground.
  • FloorAluminium tube frame, 30 mm XPS insulation and 12 mm phenolic plywood.
  • Wall and roof50 mm PIR cold-room panels with tongue-and-groove edges. They insulate and are self-supporting.
  • Front and sides12 modules with an aluminium frame and 3 mm solid polycarbonate, plus the door. They come factory-assembled.
  • InsideBathroom with shower, kitchen, water and power with quick connectors.
  • ToolsAllen keys 4, 5 and 6, a rubber mallet and a level. One 24 mm spanner for the feet.
